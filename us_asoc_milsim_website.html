About Command

The United States Army Special Operations Command (US ASOC) Roblox MILSIM generates, structures, and deploys disciplined, high-readiness Army Special Operations Forces to execute realistic tactical simulations and joint operational maneuvers.

Operating under strict chain-of-command protocols, realistic communications discipline, and comprehensive SOPs, our units deliver unmatched tactical precision.
